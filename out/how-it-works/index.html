How It Works

A clear, requirement-first sourcing process.

Every inquiry starts with your requirement, not a fixed catalogue. Here is how Munar moves it from brief to supplier-side shipment coordination.

Submit Your Requirements

The six steps

From brief to shipment.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Submit Your Requirements

    Share product type, shape and size, specification preferences, estimated quantity, packaging, and destination.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Requirement Review

    Munar reviews your brief and clarifies any open points, such as target specifications or Incoterm.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Supplier Matching

    Your requirement is matched against suitable Indonesian supply options that may be able to meet it.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Specification & Quotation

    Specifications are confirmed against the selected supplier’s actual data, and a quotation is prepared.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Order Coordination

    Once terms are agreed, Munar coordinates order communication between you and the supply side.

  6. 06

    Step 6: Supplier-Side Export & Shipment Coordination

    Munar follows up on supplier-side export preparation and shipment arrangements under the agreed terms.

Availability, specifications, and commercial terms require confirmation from the selected supplier and final agreement before any order is placed.

Transparency

A clear role, stated plainly.

International buyers deserve to know exactly who they are working with. Munar is an independent trading and sourcing partner.

What Munar does

  • Reviews and clarifies your requirement
  • Identifies Indonesian supply options that may fit
  • Coordinates specification confirmation and quotation
  • Handles order communication with the supply side
  • Follows up on supplier-side export and shipment preparation

What Munar does not claim

  • Ownership of a factory or production facility
  • Exclusive agency for any manufacturer
  • Fixed in-house grades or quality standards
  • Guaranteed stock, pricing, or lead times before supplier confirmation
